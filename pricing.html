TRANSPARENT ETIQUETTE

Studio Booking Policies

To preserve our tranquil atmosphere and respect our artists' dedicated preparation, we adhere to the following studio protocols:

Deposit Policy

A 25% credit card deposit is collected at the time of reservation to secure your suite. This deposit is credited towards your final checkout balance.

24-Hour Rescheduling

We request at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel without penalty. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit to compensate the reserved artist.

15-Minute Grace Period

If you arrive more than 15 minutes past your appointment time, we will complete as much of your service as possible in the remaining time allotted.

Refill Eligibility (40% Rule)

To qualify for infill pricing, you must have at least 40% of extensions remaining on each eye. Appointments booked past 4 weeks require a new Full Set.

Squeaky Clean Lashes

Please arrive without eye makeup, mascara, or oily moisturizers. Mascara residue prevents glue bonding and will cut into your appointment time.

Complimentary Patch Testing

If you have a history of sensitive eyes, contact dermatitis, or latex allergies, we offer a complimentary 48-hour patch test prior to your full set.
