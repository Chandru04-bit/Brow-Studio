PREPARATION GUIDE

Pre-Appointment Checklist

Follow these simple steps before arriving at our studio to ensure optimal adhesive bonding and maximum comfort.

Clean Makeup-Free Eyes

Arrive without mascara, eyeliner, or concealer. Makeup oils weaken the adhesive bond and diminish retention.

Avoid Heavy Caffeine

Skip espresso, energy drinks, or pre-workouts 2 hours prior to avoid fluttering eyelids during fine isolation work.

Remove Contact Lenses

Bring your glasses case. Keeping contacts in with your eyes closed for 90 minutes may cause eye dryness or mild irritation.

Silence Digital Devices

To maintain a peaceful sanctuary for you and other studio guests, we request silencing mobile ringtones.
