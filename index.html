COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping into our luxury lash and brow studio.

A full set of classic lashes typically takes between 90 and 105 minutes. Volume or wispy sets take approximately 120 minutes because we handcraft each individual fan during application. Refills typically take 60 to 75 minutes. We encourage you to relax, close your eyes, and enjoy a restorative lash nap!

Please arrive with squeaky-clean, makeup-free eyes and brows. Avoid waterproof mascara, oily eye creams, or serums for at least 24 hours prior. If you wear contact lenses, we recommend removing them or bringing your glasses to ensure maximum comfort during the session.

Keep your lashes clean by washing them daily with a lash-safe foaming cleanser. Gently brush them daily with your complimentary spoolie when dry. Avoid oil-based skincare, sleeping directly face-down on cotton pillowcases, and pulling or picking at your extensions. Refills are recommended every 2 to 3 weeks.

Absolutely! You are very welcome to bring reference photos. During the initial consultation, your lash specialist will inspect your natural lash health, density, and eye shape to design a customized map (e.g. Doll Eye, Cat Eye, Kitten Eye, or Kim K Wispy) that complements your natural features safely.
