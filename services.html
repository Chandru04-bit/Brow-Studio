LONGEVITY & CARE

Essential Maintenance Protocols

Protect your investment with our studio-tested aftercare advice for optimal 3–4 week retention.

The 24-Hour Rule

Avoid heavy steam, saunas, hot showers, and excessive crying during the first 24 hours to allow adhesive curing.

Oil-Free Formulations

Use only water-based cleansers. Never apply facial oils, heavy eye serums, or waterproof mascara onto extensions.

Gentle Daily Brushing

Brush lashes gently once a day when dry with your complimentary sanitized spoolie from mid-shaft to tip.

2–3 Week Refills

Natural lashes shed 3–5 hairs per eye daily. Book infills when you maintain at least 40% of your set.
