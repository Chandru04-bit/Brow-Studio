THE ATELIER STANDARD

Where Precision Science Meets High-Fashion Elegance

We reject cookie-cutter application. Every set is an intimate architectural ritual designed to preserve your natural lash health for years of uninterrupted beauty.

01

Microscopic Lash Isolation (0.5mm Margin)

Every fan is adhered strictly 0.5mm away from your eyelid. This surgical isolation allows natural follicles to shed and regrow without tension, clumping, or premature fallout.

02

Ophthalmic Hypoallergenic Adhesives

Formulated without formaldehyde, carbon black irritants, or harsh fumes. Tested and verified safe for contact lens wearers, dry eyes, and clients with heightened chemical sensitivities.

03

Golden Ratio Eye Geometry Calibration

We measure your eye distance, lid hooding, and pupil positioning to formulate a bespoke map (C, CC, D, or L curls) that visually lifts hooded eyelids and enhances facial balance.

04

Hospital-Grade Autoclave Sterilization

Instruments undergo medical-grade steam autoclave sterilization in individual pouches. Our private suites feature hospital-grade HEPA air filtration for supreme hygiene.
